# Home — extracted design tokens (TAM-62)

Source Figma (file `ipSvV1FnmzvV8TK2Ig8Aiq`), single frame: **Home + Infinite
Scroll Feed `285:3464`** (360×3685 — a scrolling feed, not a 360×800 device
frame). Shortcut cards come from the `homescreen-feature-cards` instances
`767:6580` / `767:6643` / `767:6658` / `767:6666`; the banner is component
`224:1367` instanced at `285:3507`.

Geometry + fills read from the REST tree dump (`tools/figma-export.ts tree` →
`scratch/home_root.json`) — never eyeballed. All values live in
`lib/core/theme.dart` (`AppColors.home*`, `AppHome`, `AppGradient.home*`,
`AppText.home*`). **Zero raw hex / `Color(0x…)` and zero geometry literals in
`features/home/**`**, and zero `Icons.*` (the only match is the explanatory
comment in `home_widgets.dart` saying why).

Measured proof that the render matches these numbers:
`specs/evidence/TAM-62/fidelity/render-tree/measured-geometry.md` (28/28 zones,
generated by `test/goldens/home_geometry_test.dart`).

- `285:3485` logo frame — hidden fill; only the `#FE8A02` w0.5 ring renders.
- `285:3495` help frame / `285:3552` badge glyph frame — hidden fills; the glyph
  vectors inside them are what paint.
- `285:3507` banner — `#FFFFFF` @ **0.00**: a transparent placeholder for the CMS
  image, not a white card.
- `285:3467` "Openly/Chats" header — `visible: false` **and** 412 wide (this file's

### Plus Jakarta Sans → Inter (intentional substitution)

Nodes `285:3661`/`285:3663` (mm:ss timers) and `285:3498` (avatar initial) specify
**Plus Jakarta Sans** with `letterSpacing: 0`. The app bundles only Inter + Libre
Caslon Text and disables runtime font fetching (`main.dart`), so both render Inter
at the same size/weight/line-height. The `ls: 0` **is** honoured — these two styles
deliberately override the shared ramp's −3% tracking rather than inheriting it.
Same divergence already recorded for `aartiCardSubtitle`. Every other Home string
is genuinely Inter in Figma.

## Three traps this frame sets

### 1. The carousel dots are NOT centred

Node `I285:3507;224:1357` spans x=−22645..−22613 inside a banner (`285:3507`)
spanning x=−22925..−22597 ⇒ **left inset 280, right inset 16, bottom inset 16**.
They are pinned to the banner's bottom-right corner. Centring them — the obvious
default for a carousel, and what shipped before the Phase-6 sweep — lands them
132px left of the node. Confirmed twice: by the numbers above and by the frame
render `specs/evidence/TAM-62/fidelity/figma-refs/banner-carousel.png`. Locked by
row B4 of `render-tree/measured-geometry.md`.

### 2. The shortcut cards carry TWO shadows

The card (`767:6580`) has its own #000000 @0.05 (0,+1) blur 2, **and** the grid
block that holds all four (`285:3508`) has a second, heavier #000000 @0.25 (0,+3)
blur 4. That block frame has `fills: []`, so Figma derives its shadow from the
children's alpha — i.e. it renders around each card's silhouette, which is exactly
what painting it per-card reproduces. (`showShadowBehindNode: false` is a no-op
here: the cards are opaque.) `homeShortcutGridShadow` was extracted in Phase 1 but
left unwired until the Phase-6 sweep caught it.

### 3. The playhead's shadow is its own, not the play button's

`homeAudioProgressThumbShadow` (#000000 @**0.05**, node `285:3666`) is the
playhead dot's own effect. It is easy to reach for the play button's token
instead — which is wrong twice over: that node (`285:3669`, literally named
"Play/Pause Button:shadow") is at @0.10, and it renders **nothing at all**,
because its fill is #FFFFFF @0.002 and Figma derives a drop shadow from the
layer's alpha mask. A ~zero-alpha fill casts no shadow. The frame render
(`figma-refs/feed-card-aarti.png`) confirms a bare glyph with no backing. Those
effects are vestigial ⇒ **deliberately no token**; painting them put a dark box on
the artwork that the design does not have. The pre-sweep build used the @0.10
value on the playhead — 2× the design — and is fixed.

### The MULTIPLY trap — why two PNG sets need `BlendLayer`

The shortcut artwork (`I767:6580;767:6576;767:6557` et al) and the feed card's
module tile art (`285:3545` et al) are **blend** layers: `blendMode: MULTIPLY`.
An isolated `/v1/images` render cannot bake a blend mode in — there is nothing
beneath to blend against — so both export with an **opaque white background**.
Wiring them as a plain `Image` paints a white box on the card's gradient; MULTIPLY
makes that white vanish and keeps the gold. Same trap + treatment as the Books
cover texture (TAM-76): `BlendLayer(blendMode: multiply)`
(`lib/shared/widgets/blend_layer.dart`).

### The shortcut artwork overflows its own card, by design

`767:6580` is 117 tall but its contents add to **129** (10 pad + 22 label + 12 gap
+ 85 art), and the node sets `clipsContent: true`. The design genuinely crops the
artwork's bottom ~22px. So the art is pinned to its true 113×85 via `OverflowBox`
and clipped by the card — rather than shrunk to fit, which would render every
card's art visibly smaller than the design.

### Known gaps (logged, not silently dropped)

- **Pro crown/VIP badge** — no crown/VIP node exists anywhere in the file (the
  Home frame and the unified paywall `493:3349` were both swept). Dropped from
  Phase 1 by product decision (2026-07-15); deferred to Phase 2 if the design
  owner adds a node. Per the STRICT gate, art was **not** authored to fill the
  gap.
- **Status business-status overlay** (`285:3592`–`285:3622`) — a contract gap, not
  an art gap: TAM-61's `HomeFeedItem` carries no profile fields, so rendering it
  would mean fabricating a person. The real overlay is composed from the user's
  own profile in the Status module (TAM-72).
- **Help glyph is 19.05 wide, not 20** — Figma rounds the export canvas up to
  `viewBox="0 0 20 21"` (the vector `285:3496` inks 20×20.0033), and flutter_svg
  contain-fits that into the requested 20×20 box. `BoxFit.fill` would "fix" the
  width by squashing the glyph 5% vertically, which is worse. 0.95px on a 20px
  glyph; recorded, not chased.
- **The card hairline strokes inside** — Flutter's `BoxDecoration` `Border` always
  strokes inward and insets its child, so a feed card measures 700 outer / 358
  content where Figma (`strokeAlign: CENTER`) reads 699 / 360. 1px; recorded in
  `render-tree/measured-geometry.md` rows W1/W1b.
